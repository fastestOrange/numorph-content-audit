About NuMorph AI

By Dr. Deepak Srivastava, Founder, CEO & Chief Technologist

NuMorph AI is a neuromorphic software company based in San Jose, California, delivering ERN-AI as a software-first retrofit for edge vision.

NuMorph AI is a neuromorphic software company based in San Jose, California. The company develops ERN-AI, a perception-native analog-AI software layer that brings event-driven intelligence to conventional and neuromorphic vision sensors.

The company's approach is software-first. Rather than designing new silicon and waiting for a hardware adoption cycle, NuMorph delivers neuromorphic efficiency as software on hardware that is already installed — which maximizes speed to revenue and removes the primary barrier to customer adoption.

Positioning

NuMorph AI operates as a strategic B2B partner to global tier 1–3 system integrators in edge vision and multi-modal sensing and control.

The company is complementary to, not competitive with, neuromorphic computing, hardware platform, and chip companies. ERN-AI is a software layer; it runs on the silicon those companies build and increases the addressable value of that silicon.

Industrial computer vision is growing at a double-digit CAGR through the decade.

Roadmap

The initial target is 100–1000× lower power and latency vision systems for 3-D physical AI.

PHASE 1

Fast Integration with Existing Hardware Applications

Multi-modal ERN-AI software with ALB couplings, deployed through software-first integration applications. This phase requires no hardware change from the customer and establishes ERN-AI in production environments.

PHASE 2

Clear Path to Silicon ASIC and Neuromorphic Hardware

FPGA and ASIC implementations, chips and sensors, and integration with neuromorphic chips and sensors as they mature. Multi-modal physical-AI software extends ERN-AI beyond vision.

PHASE 3

Bio-Inspired 3-D Edge Physical-AI World Models

Low-power physical-AI software and hardware solutions built around 3-D world models generated and maintained at the edge.
