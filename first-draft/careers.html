Working here

By the NuMorph AI Team

We're a small, growing neuromorphic software team in San Jose, building ERN-AI from the ground up. There are no open roles posted right now, but we're always glad to hear from exceptional people.

What we're building

NuMorph AI is a software-first company. Instead of designing new sensor hardware, we build ERN-AI (Evolutionary, Reconfigurable, Neuromorphic AI), a perception-native software layer that runs directly on digital CMOS cameras already installed in factories, warehouses, and robotic platforms. ERN-AI converts continuous frame-based video into sparse, event-driven data at the point of capture, cutting compute load by up to 500× without requiring any new sensors or silicon.

The architecture is built from modular AI-Lego-Blocks (ALB), micro-scale neuromorphic processing units modeled on the visual neurology of honeybees. At this stage we're a small team working close to the hardware, the math, and the customer problem at the same time: most people who join end up touching more than one layer of the stack.

Where we usually need people

Neuromorphic & Analog-AI Engineering

ALB and NESA architecture, spiking/event-driven models, and low-power inference: designing the processing units that make ERN-AI run efficiently on constrained edge hardware.

Computer Vision

Event-driven vision, frame-to-event conversion, and low-light and motion detection: turning conventional camera streams into the sparse representations ERN-AI needs.

Embedded & Edge Software

Device-native execution on existing CMOS camera infrastructure, plus FPGA/ASIC integration for teams building toward dedicated neuromorphic hardware.

Business & Go-to-Market

Partnerships with tier 1–3 system integrators across factory automation, machine inspection, warehouse robotics, and smart manufacturing.
