NuMorph AI · Content Audit 1

Website content audit

A review of numorph.ai's current content, and a proposal for what it should become. Work through the five steps below in order — each builds on the one before it.

Why this audit

The site's job is to explain NuMorph's technology and direction to three audiences: licensing partners and customers, investors, and the technically curious. Today it does that poorly — the writing leans on conventions that signal generated content, the visuals stand in for explanations rather than giving them, and the core ideas arrive buried under acronyms.

Rather than edit the existing pages, this audit works from first principles: establish what each section is for, agree on that, and only then write the words and design the page. The steps below follow that order deliberately.
